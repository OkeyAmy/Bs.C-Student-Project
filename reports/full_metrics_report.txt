  * This is a FULLY-SYNTHETIC demonstration corpus (560 rows seeded
    from 56 real students). It trains and evaluates on the INTENTIONAL
    synthetic feature space (incl. institution_name, qualification_type,
    programme_category) so the full pipeline can be reused when real
    data covering those columns is collected later.

    are too few for a large reliable holdout; that honest control lives
    in data/test_real.csv (11 real rows).
  * Synthetic test scores are the ground truth of the demo only.
  * CV balanced accuracies (tier ~0.86, at_risk ~0.81) are the more defensible headline numbers than single-holdout scores.
